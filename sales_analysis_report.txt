Task 2: Sales Data Analysis using Pandas

Objective

To analyze sales data using Python and Pandas.

Method

- Loaded the sales dataset using Pandas.
- Calculated total sales and average order value.
- Identified top-selling products by quantity.
- Summarized sales by category and region.
- Exported the results to a CSV file.

Findings

- Total sales: ₹296,900
- Average order value: ₹29,690
- Top-selling product by quantity: Mouse
- Highest-sales category: Electronics
- Highest-sales region: Delhi

Conclusion

Pandas helped summarize sales performance and identify leading products, categories, and regions.
